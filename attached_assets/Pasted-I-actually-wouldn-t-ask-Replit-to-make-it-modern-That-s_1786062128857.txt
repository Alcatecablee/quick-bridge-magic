I actually wouldn't ask Replit to "make it modern."

That's how you end up with glowing gradients, random floating blobs, and seventeen animations competing for attention like they're auditioning for a Marvel movie.

Instead, give it an **art direction document** with engineering constraints. Treat it like you're directing a product designer at Apple or Linear, not generating a Dribbble shot.

I'd use something like this:

---

# Prompt

You are redesigning the QuickBridge landing page.

This is **not** a reskin.

This is a complete redesign of the presentation while preserving the existing brand, copy, and functionality.

The goal is to make QuickBridge feel like a premium operating-system feature rather than a traditional SaaS website.

The visual direction should sit somewhere between:

* Apple
* Linear
* Raycast
* Arc Browser
* Vercel
* OpenAI

Avoid startup clichés.

Do NOT add rainbow gradients, glowing blobs everywhere, rotating cubes, neon effects, excessive glassmorphism, or decorative animations.

Every animation must communicate hierarchy or interaction.

---

# Core Design Philosophy

QuickBridge is no longer "a file transfer website."

It is becoming a **device continuity platform.**

The landing page should communicate one idea:

> Your devices should work together.

The page should feel calm, cinematic and intentional.

Less movement.

More meaning.

---

# Visual Direction

Use:

* Massive typography
* Lots of negative space
* Thin divider lines
* Dark background (#090909 instead of pure black)
* Hairline borders
* Soft shadows only
* Very restrained blue accent
* Subtle noise/grain texture
* Premium spacing
* Large section padding
* Rounded corners
* Clean iconography

The page should breathe.

---

# Motion Philosophy

Motion should feel expensive.

Not busy.

Never animate simply because something appeared.

Instead:

* reveal
* slide
* mask
* blur
* parallax
* timing

Everything should move with purpose.

Maximum easing quality.

No bouncing.

No spinning.

No flashy transitions.

---

# Animation Stack

Use:

* Framer Motion
* GSAP ScrollTrigger where necessary
* Lenis smooth scrolling
* Motion values for subtle parallax

Avoid heavy WebGL unless absolutely necessary.

Performance matters more than spectacle.

---

## One extra instruction I'd add

At the very end of the prompt, include this. It's surprisingly effective at preventing AI-generated "overdesign."

```text
Before writing any code, audit the existing landing page and identify which sections should be:

- Removed
- Merged
- Moved
- Redesigned
- Left unchanged

Do not start implementation immediately.

Produce a redesign plan first.

Your goal is to reduce visual complexity while increasing perceived quality.

When in doubt, remove rather than add.

The final page should feel calmer than the current version, not busier.
```

That last paragraph keeps Replit from doing what AI assistants often do: adding three animations where one excellent animation would have been enough. The hardest part of modern landing pages isn't inventing more effects. It's having the discipline to leave space, let typography lead, and make the few interactions that remain feel deliberate.
